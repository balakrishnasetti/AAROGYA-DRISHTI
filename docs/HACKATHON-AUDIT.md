# Aarohan Inter-Centre Hackathon 2026: audit and demo readiness

## Scope and baseline

This is a repository and local-demo audit, not a penetration test, clinical
validation, accessibility certification, or production-readiness approval.
The application is a simulation. Do not enter real patient information or use
its experimental model for care.

The audit covered `client/src`, `server/src`, `model-service`, the seed and
deployment configuration, the README, the architecture/security/model/API
documentation, automated checks, and a local browser route smoke test.

### Roles and principal features

Stored roles are `patient`, `doctor`, `specialist`, `health_worker`, `navigator`,
`hospital_manager`, `district_officer`, and `admin`. The UI labels these
Patient, Doctor, Specialist, Health Worker, Health Assistant, Hospital
Administrator, District Administrator, and Super Admin.

- **Patient:** OTP account, profile, consent, configurable questionnaire,
  optional guided image capture, advisory result/history, appointments/call
  requests, hospitals, diet guidance, assistant, QR, and governance requests.
- **Doctor:** assigned patient cases, consent-scoped review queue, appointment
  status, private image/history reads, feedback, signed notes, referral,
  follow-up, call inbox, and QR scan.
- **Health Worker:** catchment-scoped registration and photo capture with
  offline queue/sync.
- **Health Assistant:** referral/investigation coordination within catchment;
  not permitted to make clinical classifications.
- **Hospital Administrator:** hospital-scoped staff, roster, documents,
  bookings and appointment slots.
- **District Administrator:** aggregate, small-cell-suppressed dashboard/export.
- **Super Admin:** users/roles, facilities, audit events, and system/model
  settings.

## Five-minute safe demo script

Use only a disposable local database and the seeded synthetic accounts below.
The photo/model paths require private storage and model artifacts that are not
configured in the current environment; use the clearly labelled synthetic
case showcase for the AI-result portion instead of implying a real prediction.

| Time      | Demo                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00–0:35 | Open `/`, point out the persistent demo/clinical-service limitation, switch English/Telugu, and show patient and role sign-in entry points.                                                                                             |
| 0:35–1:20 | Open `/demo/patient-cases`; choose fictional cases to show questionnaire, retake/unclear states, advisory-only language, and simulated referral/history. Emphasize that the illustrations are not clinical photos and are not analyzed. |
| 1:20–2:05 | Sign in as a synthetic patient (development OTP), visit `/patient`, review consent controls, and show screening questionnaire progress/history. Do not upload a real photo.                                                             |
| 2:05–3:10 | Sign in as a doctor after the required initial password change. Show assigned cases, high-first review queue, the immutable original result, correction form with required reason, and call inbox.                                     |
| 3:10–3:45 | Show referral, investigation coordination, follow-up, and patient timeline/history records using synthetic data. State explicitly that biopsy/treatment lifecycle is incomplete.                                                        |
| 3:45–4:20 | Sign in as a health worker/navigator to show catchment registration/outbox and referral coordination; demonstrate online/offline indicator only if the network is intentionally toggled.                                                |
| 4:20–5:00 | Show district aggregate view, hospital operations, and admin audit/settings with their seeded role accounts. Finish with the gaps: no clinical model, no real provider-backed call test, no production deployment.                      |
